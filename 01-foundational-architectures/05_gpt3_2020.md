### 📖 Executive Abstract

> "We train GPT-3, an autoregressive language model with 175 billion parameters, 10x more than any previous non-sparse language model, and test its performance in the few-shot setting. For all tasks, GPT-3 is applied without any gradient updates or fine-tuning, with tasks specified purely via text interaction with the model."

---

### 🎯 Core Problem & Motivation

Fine-tuning models for each downstream task required thousands of labeled examples per domain, risking task-specific overfitting and poor generalization to unseen instructions.

---

### 💡 Key Innovations & Architecture

1. **Massive Scale**: Scaled autoregressive dense transformers to 175 billion parameters across 96 layers and 96 attention heads.
2. **In-Context Learning (ICL)**: Discovered that model scale unlocks the ability to perform zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot tasks without updating model weights.
3. **Diverse Pre-training Dataset**: Filtered Common Crawl combined with WebText2, Books1, Books2, and Wikipedia (300 billion tokens total).

---

### 📐 Mathematical Formulation

**Autoregressive Language Modeling Likelihood:**
$$\mathcal{L}(\theta) = - \sum_{i=1}^n \log P(x_i \mid x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{i-1}; \theta)$$

**In-Context Prompting Conditioning:**
$$P(\text{Target} \mid \text{Prompt}) = \prod_{t=1}^T P(y_t \mid x_{\text{examples}}, x_{\text{query}}, y_{<t})$$

---

### 🌟 Significance & Industry Impact

Marked the paradigm shift from task-specific fine-tuning to unified prompt engineering and foundation models. Showed that language models are general-purpose computer programs that can be guided via natural language.
