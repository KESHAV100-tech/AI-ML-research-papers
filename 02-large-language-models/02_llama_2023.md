### 📖 Executive Abstract

> "We introduce LLaMA, a collection of foundation language models ranging from 7B to 65B parameters. We train our models on trillions of tokens, and show that it is possible to train state-of-the-art models using publicly available datasets exclusively, without resorting to proprietary and inaccessible datasets."

---

### 🎯 Core Problem & Motivation

Frontier LLMs (GPT-3, PaLM, Chinchilla) were closed, proprietary, and inaccessible to researchers, stifling reproducible academic research into alignment, robustness, and interpretability.

---

### 💡 Key Innovations & Architecture

1. **Pre-normalization with RMSNorm**: Replaced LayerNorm with Root Mean Square Normalization for stable gradient flow.
2. **SwiGLU Activation Function**: Substituted standard ReLU/GELU with Swish-Gated Linear Units.
3. **Rotary Embeddings (RoPE)**: Adopted RoPE at every layer for better relative positional encoding.
4. **Public Dataset Training**: Showed that 1.4 trillion publicly available tokens could train a 13B model that outperforms GPT-3 (175B).

---

### 📐 Mathematical Formulation

**RMSNorm Normalization:**
$$\bar{a}_i = \frac{a_i}{\text{RMS}(a)} g_i, \quad \text{where } \text{RMS}(a) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{d} \sum_{i=1}^d a_i^2}$$

**SwiGLU Activation:**
$$\text{SwiGLU}(x, W, V, b, c) = \text{Swish}(x W + b) \otimes (x V + c)$$

---

### 🌟 Significance & Industry Impact

Sparked the open-source LLM explosion. LLaMA's weights became the foundation for thousands of fine-tunes (Alpaca, Vicuna, WizardLM) and local AI runtimes (llama.cpp, Ollama).
